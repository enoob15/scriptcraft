# ScriptCraft — Product Description

## What It Is

ScriptCraft is an AI-powered video script generator that writes platform-specific scripts for short-form video content. You give it a topic, a platform, a style, and a target duration. It returns a complete script — hook, body, call-to-action, hashtags, and filming tips — structured for the platform you're posting to.

The product runs as a web application with a Gemini-powered generation engine. It doesn't write generic video scripts. It writes scripts tuned for TikTok's pattern-interrupt pacing, YouTube Shorts' retention loops, Instagram Reels' visual-first framing, X's opinion-led authority, and LinkedIn's professional credibility-building. Each platform has different recommended durations, different engagement angles, and different posting times — ScriptCraft encodes all of that into the generation prompt.

Beyond the generator, ScriptCraft is a workspace. It stores your profile context (brand voice, primary goal, company), keeps your script history locally, lets you export to plain text, HTML, or print-to-PDF, and includes team seat management for the Business tier. Stripe handles billing for Pro and Business plans. The free tier gives you five scripts a month — enough to test the product on real content before paying.

## Why It Exists

Writing video scripts that perform well on social platforms is a specific skill. Most founders, marketers, and creators don't have it. The people who do have it charge real money — $200-500 per script, or retainer arrangements that start at $2,000/month.

The alternative is writing scripts yourself. But a good short-form script isn't just talking into a camera. It's a hook in the first two seconds, pacing that holds attention through a 15-90 second runtime, a structure that pays off the promise of the hook, and a CTA that matches the platform's culture. TikTok audiences scroll past anything that doesn't grab them instantly. LinkedIn audiences expect authority and business value. X audiences want punchy opinions. Writing for all five platforms well requires understanding five different content cultures.

Existing AI writing tools don't solve this well. Jasper and Copy.ai are general-purpose copywriting platforms that happen to have video script templates. They produce generic scripts that read like blog copy with a hook bolted on. They don't know that TikTok rewards pattern interrupts and relatability, that YouTube Shorts needs retention loops, that Reels needs visual polish, that X needs opinion-led framing, or that LinkedIn needs business credibility. ScriptCraft encodes platform-specific strategy into the generation prompt and produces scripts that are shaped for the platform, not just labeled with its name.

The gap in the market: a tool that treats video script generation as platform-specific craft, not generic text generation with a "video script" template. ScriptCraft fills that gap.

### Generating a TikTok script

You open the ScriptCraft workspace. You fill in:

```
Topic: Why most freelance designers underprice their work
Platform: TikTok
Style: Educational
Duration: 45 seconds
Target audience: Freelance designers
Key points:
  - 92% charge below market rate
  - Pricing fear comes from portfolio gaps
  - Value-based pricing fixes the gap
Brand voice: Smart, direct, clear, and creator-friendly
Primary goal: Drive saves, shares, and qualified inbound interest
```

ScriptCraft returns:

```
Title: Why 92% of Freelance Designers Underprice Their Work

Hook:
92% of freelance designers charge less than they're worth.
Here's why — and how to fix it in one conversation with yourself.

Body:
[beat] Most designers price based on fear. What if the client says no?
What if I lose the project? So they drop the number.
[cut] But the client said no because the number was low, not because
the work was bad. Low pricing signals low confidence.
[show proof] I tracked 50 design proposals over six months. The ones
priced 40% higher had a 60% higher close rate. Clients read price
as a proxy for quality.
[beat] Switch to value-based pricing. What is the work worth to the
client's business? A redesign that increases conversion by 15% is
worth $50,000 to a company making $330,000 a year. Price accordingly.

CTA:
Save this video. Then go to the link in my bio and use the free
pricing calculator. It takes 90 seconds and tells you what to charge.

Hashtags:
#freelancedesigner #pricingstrategy #designbusiness #valuebasedpricing
#freelancetips #creativebusiness #pricing

Filming Tips:
1. Open with the stat on screen — text overlay with "92%" in bold
2. Use a two-camera setup: one straight-on, one at 45 degrees
3. Cut between cameras on each [beat] marker to maintain pace
4. Screen-record the pricing calculator for the CTA section
5. Keep total runtime under 50 seconds for TikTok's algorithm
```

### Platform differences

The same topic generates different scripts for different platforms:

**TikTok (45s, educational):** Fast hook with a stat, pacing markers for visual cuts, CTA drives to a tool.

**LinkedIn (90s, educational):** Opens with an observation about the market, builds authority through a framework, CTA invites connection and discussion. Longer, more formal, structured around business value.

**X/Twitter (45s, opinion-led):** Punchy, contrarian opening. "Most designers price like they're afraid of money." Short, direct, designed for reposts and quote-tweets.

**YouTube Shorts (40s, educational):** Retention-loop structure — opens with the payoff promise, delivers in segments, ends with a teaser for the next video.

**Instagram Reels (30s, entertaining):** Visual-first framing, trend remixable, aspirational tone. Shorter, punchier, designed for saves and shares.

### Export workflow

## How It Works

1. **Set up your workspace.** Enter your name, company, brand voice, and primary goal. This context carries into every script you generate — you don't re-explain your brand each time.

2. **Enter your script brief.** Provide a topic, select a platform (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, LinkedIn), choose a style (educational, entertaining, promotional, storytelling, trending), set a duration, and optionally add key points and a target audience.

3. **Generate.** ScriptCraft sends your brief to Gemini with a platform-optimized prompt that encodes the platform's engagement angle, recommended duration, and content culture. The response comes back as structured JSON: title, hook, body, CTA, hashtags, filming tips.

4. **Review and refine.** The script appears in the workspace. You can read it, copy it, or export it. If the topic or approach needs adjustment, regenerate with different inputs. Your script history is stored locally for reference.

5. **Export.** Copy to clipboard, download as plain text, download as formatted HTML, or print to PDF. The HTML export produces a clean, styled document suitable for client review.

6. **Upgrade for volume.** Free gives you five scripts a month. Pro ($9/mo) gives you unlimited generation, advanced script analytics, and priority Gemini processing. Business ($29/mo) adds shared team seats, a business analytics dashboard, and customer portal billing controls.
