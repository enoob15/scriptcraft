# ScriptCraft Product Copy

## Product Description (short)

ScriptCraft generates platform-optimized video scripts for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, and LinkedIn. Feed it a rough topic and get a structured script with hooks, pacing, and CTA logic — ready to record.

---

## Hero Section

### Headline Options

**Option A (selected): "Turn a rough idea into a video script ready to record."**
- Rationale: Describes the transformation in customer language. Creators don't think in terms of "AI generation" — they think about going from idea to script. Specific (rough idea → ready to record) without being clever.

**Option B: "Stop staring at a blank page. Get a video script in seconds."**
- Rationale: Names the pain directly (blank page paralysis) and the speed benefit. Good for ad traffic where the pain point is already top of mind.

**Option C: "Video scripts tuned for the platform you're posting to."**
- Rationale: Leads with the differentiator (platform-optimized output). Stronger for audiences who've tried generic AI writers and been disappointed by generic output.

### Subheadline (for Option A)

ScriptCraft writes hooks, pacing, and CTA structures for TikTok, Shorts, Reels, X, and LinkedIn — so you spend less time writing and more time recording.

## Copy Principles Applied

- **Clarity over cleverness**: The headline states the product's core transformation in plain language.
- **Benefits over features**: "Ready to record" is the benefit; "AI generation" is just the mechanism.
- **Specific over vague**: Names the actual platforms (TikTok, Shorts, Reels, X, LinkedIn) and script components (hooks, pacing, CTA structures).
- **Customer language**: "Rough idea," "ready to record," "blank page" — language creators actually use.
- **One idea per section**: The hero communicates one thing — idea in, script out.
- **No exclamation points**: Confident and direct.
- **No buzzwords**: Avoids "innovative," "streamline," "optimize," "revolutionize."
- **CTA communicates value**: "Generate Your First Script" tells them what they get and that it's action-oriented.
